📖 User Guide & Knowledge Base (පරිශීලක මාර්ගෝපදේශය)

Learn how to manage coconut procurement, grading, husk transformation, sales invoicing, and double-entry accounting.

🥥 Go to Dashboard
1️⃣ Raw Coconut Procurement (අමු පොල් මිලදී ගැනීම්)
Count Basis

• Navigate to Coconut Purchase.
• Select your supplier (or click "+ Add" to create one on the fly), purchase date, and coconut category (Grade A, Medium, Small, or Ungraded).
• Enter the nut count and rate per coconut. If freight charges were paid, enter the Transport Cost.
• DigiBiz calculates the Weighted Average Landed Cost and automatically updates your Raw Coconut inventory and General Ledger (1-1040-01).

2️⃣ Raw Husk Procurement (පොල් ලෙලි මිලදී ගැනීම්)
Weight Basis (Kg)

• Navigate to Husk Purchase.
• Enter the weight in kilograms (or convert MT into kg) and the unit rate per kg along with transport.
• The system capitalizes your live Husk Stock on Yard at weighted average cost.

3️⃣ Grading & Sorting (පොල් වර්ගීකරණය)
Value Optimization

• When truckloads of ungraded coconuts arrive, go to Coconut Grading.
• Select the source batch (e.g. Ungraded) and input the sorted quantities for Grade A (Good), Grade B (Medium), and Grade C (Low).
• If damaged/rotten nuts are discovered, enter them in the Discarded field. DigiBiz re-allocates stock and writes off spoilage losses to 5-5010-01.

4️⃣ Value Addition & Production Runs (නිෂ්පාදන ක්‍රියාවලිය)
Manufacturing

• Go to Production to convert yard husks into Husk Chips, Coir Fibre Bales, or Grow Bags.
• Choose a recipe, enter the output quantity, and confirm raw husk kilograms consumed.
• Add any direct machine labor/electricity costs. DigiBiz automatically deducts raw husks and adds finished product stock with accurate capitalized unit costs.

5️⃣ Sales & Wholesale Invoicing (විකුණුම් සහ ඉන්වොයිස්)
Point of Sale

• Go to Sales to issue invoices for both whole coconuts and finished goods.
• Build a multi-item cart. DigiBiz tracks real-time Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) and instant profit margins.
• If sold on Credit, customer receivables are automatically updated, with statements available in Ledgers.

6️⃣ General Ledger & Accounting Invariants
Double-Entry

• Check Accounting for real-time Trial Balance verification (\(\text{Total Debits} = \text{Total Credits}\)), Statement of Profit & Loss, and Balance Sheet.
• Track liquid cash flow, bank transfers, and loans under Finance Ledger and Loans.
