Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new sale?
Go to Point of Sale module, search for products, add to cart, and complete payment.
How to add new products?
Navigate to Inventory → Add Product, fill in product details and save.
How to generate reports?
Go to Reports section, select date range and report type, then generate.
How to manage staff users?
Super Admin can add/edit/delete users from Admin Dashboard.
How to backup data?
Go to Settings → Backup tab, click "Backup Now" button.
How to change subscription plan?
Contact support via WhatsApp or email for subscription changes.
